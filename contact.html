Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Contact Questions

Quick answers to common questions about visiting, drop-offs, and admissions inquiries.

Can I drop by for a tour without booking in advance?

To ensure campus security and uninterrupted classroom learning, all tours must be scheduled at least 24 hours in advance. You can easily book online using our tour scheduler above!

What are your main front desk office hours?

Our concierge desk is staffed Monday through Friday from 7:00 AM to 6:30 PM. Email inquiries sent after hours are answered early the next business morning.

How do I contact my child's teacher during the day?

Enrolled parents can send direct messages anytime via our Live Parent Mobile App. For urgent matters, call the main reception desk and we will relay your message immediately.

Where do parents park during morning drop-off?

We provide 15 reserved express drop-off parking bays right outside our security gates with staff attendants assisting toddlers from cars between 7:30 AM and 9:00 AM.
