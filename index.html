About LittleStars

Nurturing Young Minds Through Love, Play & Science

We believe early childhood is the bedrock of lifelong curiosity. Our early learning philosophy combines hands-on play, sensory exploration, emotional security, and structured STEM activities to help your child thrive.

100% Safe & Secure Campus

Biometric entry, monitored play zones & strict safety protocols.

Nutritious Chef-Prepared Meals

Our Curriculum

Tailored Learning Programs For Every Stage

Explore our stage-by-stage learning pathways designed for holistic early development.

01

Infant Care Nest

0 - 1.5 Years

Gentle, loving care focused on sensory exploration, tummy time, motor milestones, and personalized feeding schedules.

02

Toddler Explorers

1.5 - 3 Years

Active discovery encouraging language growth, motor skills, wooden building blocks, and social play.

03

Preschool Adventurers

3 - 5 Years

Structured early learning preparing children for kindergarten with phonics, math concepts, science, and music.

04

After-School Club

5 - 10 Years

Enriching after-school environment with homework assistance, STEM robotics projects, sports, and creative crafts.
