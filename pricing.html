Tuition FAQ

Frequently Asked Financial Questions

Clear answers regarding payment schedules, deposits, and vacation policies.

How does billing work?

Tuition is billed on an automated weekly or monthly schedule via secure ACH transfer or credit card on the 1st of each period.

Is there a vacation credit policy?

Yes! Every enrolled family receives 2 weeks of 50% tuition credit per year for planned family vacation time.

What is required to reserve a spot?

A $150 deposit holds your child's spot, which is credited directly toward your first week of tuition upon enrollment.
